  intercept: 14.7, the expected "marks" when every number column is 0 and every group column is at its baseline.

  breakfast "yes" against "no": "marks" is 3.28 higher (95 % CI 0.698 to 5.87); unlikely to be chance (p = 0.013).
Method: multiple imputation by chained equations (predictive mean matching with 5 donors, seed=1): the empty cells were filled in 20 times, the model was fitted to each of the 20 filled-in tables, and the results were pooled with Rubin's rules (t-based intervals with Barnard-Rubin degrees of freedom), as R pool(with(mice(...), lm(marks ~ hours + sleep + breakfast))).
The filling-in adds uncertainty: up to 6 % of the variance of an effect comes from the differences between the filled-in tables. Tests for whole group columns are not pooled here.
