Made 2 clusters of 30 rows from 10 columns (each put on the same scale first): sizes 14 and 16 (clusters 1 and 2, numbered in the order they first appear). 24.4 % of the spread lies between the clusters (between-cluster / total sum of squares); the rest is within them.
Method: k-means with 25 random starts (seed=1); each start runs Lloyd's algorithm and then Hartigan's single-point moves, and the start with the smallest within-cluster sum of squares is kept (R kmeans(nstart = 25) uses Hartigan-Wong and reaches the same kind of solution). Added the column "cluster".
